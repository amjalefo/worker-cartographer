# open-questions.md — template

Audit step 6 writes this. Copy it into the map you produce; it is not optional and it is not a place for polish.

Everything the closed card set could not hold goes here, plus everything the instrument itself got wrong on this walk. A map with no open questions is either a very small territory or a cartographer who stopped looking.

---

## Things that fit no card type

For each: what it is, where it lives, and why none of the six nouns or six movements holds it. Do not invent a thirteenth type — `reference.md` says the closed set is closed, and this file is where that promise gets paid.

- **{thing}** — `{path}` — {why no type fits}

## Rule collisions hit on this walk

- **{rule A} vs {rule B}** — {which won, and on what grounds}
